New SPS Ballon d'Or System

The old Ballon d'Or-specific trophy and award scoring system has been removed. A player's Ballon d'Or total is simply the sum of the points earned in every competition played during the season.

Total = League + Cups + International + Every Other Competition

League Competition

Club finishes 1st+5
Club finishes 2nd+4
Club finishes 3rd+3
Club finishes 4th+2
Club finishes 5th+1
Every player in the club receives the points for the club's final league position.

Cup / Knockout Competition

Round of 16+1
Quarter-final+2
Semi-final+3
Final+4
Winner+5
The score is based on the furthest stage reached. The winner receives +5 for winning the competition.

Penalty Saves×5
Goals×2

Successful dribbles remain an offensive/midfield stat; successful tackles remain the defender performance stat, following the normal SPS rules.

Special Awards

Golden Boot×3
Assist Leader×3
MVP×2
Cup Final MVP+5
All MVP awards received during the season are counted in their respective competitions.

Ballon d'Or Calculation

Each competition is scored separately. After every competition has been scored, all of that player's competition totals for the season are added together. The player with the highest total wins the SPS Ballon d'Or.
